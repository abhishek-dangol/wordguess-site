SupportPrivacy policy

The short version

Word Guess has no accounts, no ads and no analytics. We do not collect your name, email, contacts, location or photos, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.

Effective date: November 8, 2026.

What stays on your device

Your settings, team names, last game choices and whether you have unlocked the full game are saved on your phone only. We never receive them. Deleting the app deletes them.

Purchases

The one-time unlock is sold through Apple. Apple handles your payment, and we never see your card or Apple account details.

To confirm that a purchase unlocked the game and to restore it on a new phone, the app uses a service called RevenueCat. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app identifier, your purchase receipt, and basic device and app information (such as app version and country). It does not receive your name or email. RevenueCat's own policy is at revenuecat.com/privacy.

App updates

The app can download small content and bug-fix updates through a service called Expo. To deliver an update, that service receives the app version, the device platform and the network address of the request. It is not used to identify you.

Children

Word Guess is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. The app shows no ads and has no chat or social features.

Names in the game

Some cards use brand, company or celebrity names as words to guess. They are plain text only. Word Guess is not affiliated with or endorsed by any of them.
