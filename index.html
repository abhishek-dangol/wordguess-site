Common questions

What is free?
Decks 1 to 4 (1,200 cards) are free. A one-time purchase unlocks every deck and category, including ones added later. There is no subscription.

I bought the unlock on another device or reinstalled the app.
Open Settings, tap Restore purchase, and use the same Apple account you bought with.

The purchase did not unlock anything.
Check your internet connection and tap Restore purchase in Settings. If it still does not work, email us with the time of the purchase and we will help.

Does the game need internet?
No. Playing works offline. Buying or restoring the unlock needs a connection.

I found a wrong or offensive card.
Email us the word and we will fix it in the next update.

How do refunds work?
Apple handles refunds. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.
